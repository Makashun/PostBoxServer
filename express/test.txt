GET Method

Retrieve all records
http://localhost:8888/

Retrieve a records by District
http://localhost:8888/district/{districtName)
eg: Central, Southern, North,灣仔
Optional query:
http://localhost:8888/district/Central
http://localhost:8888/district/Central?day=weekday&after=09:00
http://localhost:8888/district/Central?day=weekday&before=13:00
http://localhost:8888/district/Central?day=saturday&after=09:00&before=13:00

Retrieve a records by Region
http://localhost:8888/region/{regionName}
eg: Kowloon, HONG KONG, 九龍, 港島
Optional query:
http://localhost:8888/region/KOWLOON
http://localhost:8888/region/KOWLOON?day=weekday&after=09:00
http://localhost:8888/region/KOWLOON?day=weekday&before=13:00
http://localhost:8888/region/KOWLOON?day=saturday&after=09:00&before=13:00

Retrieve a records by Location
http://localhost:8888/location/{locationName}
eg:Chatham Road, Ping Shek Estate, 锦田公路
Optional query:
http://localhost:8888/location/Chatham
http://localhost:8888/location/Chatham?day=weekday&after=09:00
http://localhost:8888/location/Chatham?day=weekday&before=13:00
http://localhost:8888/location/Chatham?day=saturday&after=09:00&before=13:00

Retrieve a record by ID
http://localhost:8888/id/{id}

DELETE Method
Delete a record by ID
http://localhost:8888/{id}
